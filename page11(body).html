Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I moisturize?

Daily, preferably after shower.

Can I exfoliate daily?

No, 1–2 times a week is ideal.

Is body oil better than lotion?

Both are beneficial; oils seal moisture, lotions hydrate.

Should body care change with seasons?

Yes, heavier products in winter, lighter in summer.
