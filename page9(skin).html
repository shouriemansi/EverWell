Know Your Skin Type

Different Skin Types

Oily Skin

Oily skin produces excess sebum making the face shiny. It is prone to clogged pores and acne. Makeup may slide off quickly. Requires light products. Gel-based cleansers work best. Avoid heavy creams.

Dry Skin

Dry skin feels tight and rough. It may appear flaky or dull. Fine lines show easily. Needs rich moisturizers. Gentle cleansers are recommended. Avoid harsh soaps. Hydration is crucial. Oils and creams help restore moisture.

Combination Skin

Combination skin has both oily and dry areas. Usually oily T-zone and dry cheeks. Needs balanced care. Lightweight products work well. Avoid over-cleansing. Hydration should be even.

Acne Prone Skin

Acne-prone skin is more likely to develop pimples, blackheads, whiteheads, and painful breakouts. This skin type often produces excess oil. Hormonal changes, stress, improper skincare, and diet can worsen acne. Acne-prone skin requires gentle, non-comedogenic products.

Normal Skin

Normal skin is well-balanced and healthy. It is neither too oily nor too dry and usually has a smooth texture with minimal imperfections. Skin maintains good hydration levels. A simple, consistent skincare routine helps maintain its natural glow and strength.
