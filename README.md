# 𐙚 EverWell

A personal wellness and productivity platform designed to help you stay organized, build healthy habits, practice self-care, and maintain balance in your daily life.

## About

EverWell brings together everyday productivity tools and self-care guidance in one place — so you don't have to juggle five different apps to plan your day and take care of yourself.

## Features

### Organize
- **Journal** — Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly journalling with a view of past entries
- **Notes** — Quick note-taking with saved past entries
- **To-Do List** — Weekly to-do list to manage tasks one day at a time
- **Reminders** — Reminders paired with a calendar view

### Track
- **Mood Tracker** — Log how you're feeling day to day

### Self-Care Guides
- **Skincare** — Skincare routine tips and guidance
- **Hair Care** — Hair care tips, a daily routine, product essentials, and FAQs
- **Body Care** — A 5-step body care routine, product essentials, and FAQs

### Account & Navigation
- Sign-up page
- Notifications page
- Responsive navigation across all pages (Home, Notifications, About, Sign-Up)
